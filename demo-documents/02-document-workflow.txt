NimbusDesk fictional demo help centre — Document workflow
This document contains synthetic rules for a local demonstration.

A document begins as a draft. An Editor reviews its title, content and source links before publication. Published documents are visible to Viewers. Editing a published document creates a new draft revision; the previous published revision remains visible until the new revision is published. Archiving hides the document from normal search but retains its revision history for Owners and Editors.
